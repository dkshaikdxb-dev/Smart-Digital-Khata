# Smart Digital Khata - Product Requirements Document

# Product Overview
Smart Digital Khata is a modern ledger and local commerce platform designed for small merchants, retailers, and service providers.

The platform enables merchants to:
- Track customer dues
- Send payment reminders
- Manage family-linked accounts
- Monitor collections
- Build customer relationships

---

# Target Users

- Local Retail Shops
- Service Centers
- Appliance Repair Shops
- Electronics Retailers

## Secondary Users
